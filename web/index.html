What is BitVoid?

Bitcoin wastes energy 24/7 and takes 10 minutes per block. BitVoid only uses energy when there's actual demand, confirms in seconds, and pays miners forever.

Proof of Energy

Energy is measured and recorded per block. Not wasted on useless hashes. Every coin is backed by real watts.

Event-Driven Blocks

Blocks are produced when the network needs them. 3 to 60 seconds depending on traffic. Not a fixed clock.

Infinite Supply

No cap. No halvings. Miners are always paid. Ramped emission starts low and grows over 4 years, then stabilizes.

Quantum-Safe

Post-quantum signatures from day one. ML-DSA (Dilithium). Immune to future quantum attacks.

Fair Launch

No premine. No dev fund. No instamine. Same rules for everyone. Announce first, mine second.

CPU Mineable

Starts CPU-friendly. No ASIC advantage early on. Normal PCs can mine from day one.

How to mine

1. Download

Get bitvoid-core from GitHub releases. Put it in a folder.

2. Run

Double-click mine.bat (Windows) or run ./mine.sh (Linux/Mac). That's it.

3. Earn

Your PC mines blocks and earns BTVD. The script handles everything. No config files, no command line.
